Five scanner candidates across five brands overall; three in batch one: FOXWELL NT301 for engine/emissions code reading, Innova 5610 for supported handheld bidirectional diagnosis, and Autel MK900-BT for wireless tablet diagnosis and documented CAN FD/DoIP support. Batch-two BlueDriver and LAUNCH slots are provisional. These are useful capability tiers, not a brand quota or an assertion of equal functions.
