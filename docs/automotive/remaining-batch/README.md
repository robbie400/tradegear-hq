## Publication checks still open

- **Image permissions: 0 of 39 documented.** The pack identifies actual manufacturer/retailer sources and retains permission-evidence fields. A public product gallery is not proof of a reuse licence. No photo is represented as licensed and none is imported into the live site.
- **Photo resolution:** 33 source photos are at least 1,000 pixels on the longest edge. OTC 6295, OTC 6297 and Lisle 60900 are 800–999 pixels. OTC 6007 (453 × 450), Streamlight 68750 (600 × 600) and Mityvac MV6412A (270 × 270) need larger replacements for the full review hero. A model-specific 1,357 × 1,500 O'Reilly MV6412A image is located in its record, but direct binary download returned HTTP 403. Never upscale the small file and call it high resolution. The yellow-only Streamlight image replaces a generic manufacturer photo showing two colours.
- **Current Motivx variants:** approve MX2320-US (64.3 mm, 14 flutes) and MX2326-US (86.6 mm, 16 flutes) before implementation. Their stable tracker IDs remain unchanged. MX2326's older 86.4 mm description is not used for the current model.
- **Amazon package caveats:** 81206T and 9533N have matching manufacturer-part fields but conflicting/legacy secondary model fields. The product notes preserve these discrepancies. BlueDriver LSB2 is explicitly matched in Amazon's identity fields; the generic manufacturer Pro page alone does not identify its hardware generation. Recheck selected sellers, contents and these distinctions immediately before publication.
- **Safety/application checks:** verify current First Alert service/recall notices; do not infer clearance from an empty search. For pressure tools, engine testers and pressing/pulling equipment, match adapters and operating procedures to the vehicle. No hands-on testing is claimed.
- **Editorial completion:** these are preparation notes, not replacement full reviews. Substantial equipment must receive the established full-review hero, verdict, best/skip use cases, sourced pros/cons, detailed analysis, alternatives, FAQs, sources and buying CTA. No copied Amazon ratings, counts or quotations have been added.

The new coverage completes C11 (mechanical pullers/suspension) and C15 (cooling), and adds W07–W09 to the existing plans: 20 comparisons and 10 workflows in total. Group different operations rather than ranking unrelated tools against one another. OTC 6295 already includes 6297; present the set and standalone separator as alternatives.
