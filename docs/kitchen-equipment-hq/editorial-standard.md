# Editorial/visual standard

Refer to **`kitchenmatch-concept.md`** for the signature interactive experience and **`audience-hubs.md`** for the audience taxonomy.

## Core review guide

Reviewed product pages should match the established TradeGear HQ `components/FullProductReview.tsx`: product photo and byline, Our Take, specific best-for / skip-if, pros and cons, key manufacturer specs, four or more in-depth model-specific sections where evidence warrants it, FAQs, alternatives, sources, and clear affiliate CTA. Original interpretative research, not hands-on claims. Warm amber/cream panels for **sourced insights**; no invented chef or customer quote, duplicate images, unsourced stars or price.

## Comprehensive guide template

Each measured long-tail keyword targets a unique editorial answer: buyer scenario, comparison of 4–6 verified choices, evidence-backed matrix with use-case constraints, individual model summaries, real tradeoffs, "what not to buy", owners' concerns when sourceable, manufacturer references and internal links. For espresso under $500, budget means machine-only unless headline says full setup; disclose grinder cost. For mixers, don't infer duty cycle from watts. For appliance clearance, unknown means **check**, not "fits". For any residential-to-commercial recommendation, require direct manufacturer proof.

## Visual identity

Deep navy `#152235`, warm paper `#FFFCF6`, muted espresso brown `#744B32`, warm quote cards `#FFF6E9` with borders `#D5A36D`, amber action `#D98134`; approve actual palette on preview. Premium editorial magazine meets decision calculator rather than a clone of Amazon's visual identity. Genuine licensed kitchen photography; 3–4 purposeful illustrative breaks on long guides; accessible responsive data tables and icons. Social proof only where real and permitted. Three design examples before scaling.

## Quality gates

No unverified model/variant or Amazon destination, no unlicensed manufacturer assets/ratings, no fabricated reviews. Distinguish manufacturer claims from original analysis. Source IDs and checked dates for each technical claim. Confirm model availability in the US and warranty terms. All indexable content must be useful beyond the keyword and substantially different from related pages.
