## How KitchenMatch handles premium products
A person who says "I blend frozen fruit for two shakes a week" should **not** be upsold to a $900 all-in-one kitchen system solely because affiliate commission is larger. Recommend a sensible class of product and **explicitly show when premium ownership benefits are unjustified**. A person who needs two 1kg dough mixes daily should be warned about unsupported home-mixer duty claims; commercial branch requires service support, actual certified equipment and supplier research.

## Profit, ethics and data limits
No candidate has verified Amazon.com ASIN, retail link, reuse-cleared official photo or current price API access. Do not automatically populate product pages or commissioned rankings from this register. The manufacturer's "Use: Household" spec trumps the desire to place it in the pro-kitchen path. Revenue calculations use actual Amazon product classification, not category assumptions.
