# Development sequence, QA and business validation
## Target pricing and intent gate
- Apply [premium selection policy](./premium-positioning.md): main editorial range **$300–$1,500+** and approximate **$200** featured-product floor, permitting necessary honest exceptions. Do not create cheap gadgets/low-price SEO pages just to chase search volume.
- V1 **Kitchen Coach = original editorial tips and rules-based KitchenMatch**, not a paid AI chatbot or invented human chef. The same verified data powers both; no per-query API cost.

## Stage 0 — validate opportunity and market lane
- Compare US organic search-result composition on at least 20 priority query SERPs (not just paid ad competition). Classify lab-tested publishers, specialist affiliate sites, retailers, Reddit, shopping packs, rich results. Record evidence, date, domain authority as appropriate. Identify 3–5 defensible gaps (e.g. exact countertop fit, full espresso setup spend, professional duty boundaries).
- Expand Keyword Planner for premium `best espresso machine under 1000`, `best espresso grinder`, `best chamber vacuum sealer for home use`, `best stand mixer for bread dough`, `commercial countertop mixer`, `best commercial blender`, `best commercial espresso machine`. Mark all new volume ranges `UNMEASURED` until confirmed.
- Audit Amazon.com listing reality and rates; verify rights to product photography. Confirm working domain/brand availability after user approves name. No site purchase/paid SaaS/ads without explicit approval.
## Stage 1 — information architecture and proof of value
- Create separate `kitchen-equipment-hq` repo (after owner approval), port neutral TradeGear review/guide modules under new brand.
- Implement 3 cornerstone **finished, PREMIUM** buying guides and 12–15 fully researched reviews (espresso machines under $500 with $200 minimum model floor and budget caveats; premium air fryer toaster oven combos; $300+ stand mixers for bread) as a Vercel **noindex preview**. No blanket low-AOV product review catalogue.
- Implement `KitchenMatch Fit Finder` v1 for those 3 workflows only: 4–6 simple inputs, explainable results and skip/alternate advice. It must make a materially different correct recommendation for different needs and send users back into canonical research content. No signup.
- Preview UX with real mobile viewport and desktop, no mock listings, no unsupported claims, image license record.
## Stage 2 — broader commercial content foundation
- Complete premium **nugget ice maker**, **chamber vacuum sealer for home use**, and one validated **premium espresso / blender-and-prep system** guide (not an automatically approved cheap protein-shake blender roundup). Confirm Google Ads keyword bands and organic SERP feasibility for any unmeasured premium target before writing; add 12–20 additional verified substantial reviews.
- Launch **six** useful buyer setup articles (espresso station, sourdough/bread, compact apartment kitchen, freezer meal prep, small household, entertaining/home bar).
- 3–6 unique comparisons only where source-backed and distinct. 4–6 categories and cook profile hubs with substantial value, no duplication.
- QA guide <> review <> category <> fit result links, 404, metadata, breadcrumbs/schema, LCP/CLS/performance, proper AMP? No AMP needed; check mobile size, contrast, consent/cookies/privacy and schema. Crawl on preview. Label real data.
## Stage 3 — publish only with explicit approval
- Affiliate approval/register new site and valid Amazon.com tracking. Verify every CTA and the required statement, no unsupported star rating.
- Switch verified, indexed public pages; submit sitemap, connect GSC/GA4, monitor 7- and 28-day performance and conversion events.
- Continue next 8–12 long-tail keyword pages only after SERP/keyword validation; refresh winners based on data.
## Metrics
- Coverage: verified product specs, ASINs, research citations and images; nonduplicate canonicals; 0 orphan public pages.
- Usability: >3 distinct functional KitchenMatch cases, actionable download/share decision, no fabricated model fit.
- Search: impressions/pages at positions 1–20; qualified organic visits; don't infer success from indexing alone.
- Money: verified attributed affiliate clicks, qualifying commissions and earnings per 100 unique search visitors; avoid projections without conversion evidence.
## Risk register
- SEO: highly competitive reviews with major tested publishers -> compete on fitting user scenarios + measured clearances/data provenance.
- Paid-search metric confusion: GKP "High" is **ads competition**, not keyword difficulty; bands don't give exact traffic prediction.
- Revenue: Amazon rate/category classification and cookie window, residential premium gear low purchase volume; no assumed conversion.
- Professional product compliance: certification, sanitation, electrical supply, warranty/safety, supplier availability; commercial hub research-only until documented.
- Product pages: manufacturer specs vs editorial synthesis; no imaginary lab tests, chef identities, review stars, or customer reviews. Quote permission, media rights.
- Cannibalization: budget/capacity keyword overlap; explicit content registry primary ownership.
- Tech: never deploy initial new brand inside live TradeGear HQ; new Vercel project/GA4/GSC/Amazon registration after confirmation.
