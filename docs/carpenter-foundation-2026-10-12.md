# Carpenters & Joiners — first review batch

## Scope

One hub, eight distinct job buying plans, four five-product comparisons and twenty carpenter-context product assessments. Eleven models are new to the catalogue; nine shared drills/drivers retain their documented identity but get original carpenter-specific buying analysis. Existing routes remain available.

Workflows: apprentice starter kit, framing, finish trim, door installation, cabinet installation, decking, renovation and cut-station/battery setup. Every workflow includes a job sequence, starter/budget/upgrade guidance, explicit equipment omissions and linked manufacturer documentation.

Comparisons: circular saws, miter saws, drills and impact drivers. Products have model-specific photos, labelled research-based assessments and Amazon.com model-search links with the existing Associates tag. Search destinations are deliberately search URLs, not claimed verified single-product listings. No live price, stock, bestseller or customer-rating claims were added.

Makita marks XDT19Z discontinued and identifies XDT20Z as replacement. The new carpenter comparison uses XDT20Z. Existing trades' XDT19Z references need a separate editorial update; this batch does not silently replace their models or reviews.

Manufacturer photo endpoints for two Makita saws could not be fetched in this environment. Exact-model retailer photos are used and credited instead. Product documentation remains the manufacturer source.

## Toolkit builder — later phase

Questions: trade; job type; apprentice/experienced role; budget; what tools are already owned; exact battery platform; corded power access; portability or station-space constraints; material/stock size; occasional versus recurring workload.

Use deterministic catalogue rules to recommend essential items, optional upgrades and alternatives. Explain every choice. Avoid recommending two alternatives as if both are required. Include blades, bits, batteries, chargers, support and dust-control dependencies. A user-entered budget should produce a priority plan until sufficiently current, permitted retailer pricing is available; it must not imply an accurate live total from guessed prices.

Existing Amazon click analytics can record trade, product and builder placement when that feature is implemented. No toolkit widget is included in this first batch.
